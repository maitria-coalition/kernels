## Honest readings

- Wins are real on both targets but modest at the largest size: the
  scan is memory-bandwidth-bound once the slab leaves cache (the 5080-
  class "SIMD makes it 8x" intuition does not survive contact with a
  streaming i64 scan, and these tables say so). The cache-resident
  regime — where per-certificate coefficient tables actually live — is
  where the 2–4x sits.
- Single-run medians on otherwise-loaded boxes; read as "which horse
  won and by roughly how much," not tight statistics.
- The reference is a decent compiler-optimized scalar loop, not a
  strawman: early-exit search loops do not autovectorize, which is
  exactly why a hand lane pays here at all.
