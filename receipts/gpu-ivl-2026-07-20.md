## Honest readings (what licenses what)

- **The enclosure fold costs ~what the s64 fold costs, per entry.**
  ivl matmul 256^3 fresh (65K entries × red 256, with 8 directed
  products + 6 min/max + 2 directed adds per reduction step) lands
  at 0.995 ms where the s64 kernel's 512^3 (8× the entries, 2× the
  reduction) lands at 2.2 ms — the rows-as-data decode dominates
  both; the interval arithmetic's 8× multiply count is not the
  bottleneck at this grain. There is NO XLA comparison row for the
  interval lanes and there never will be: no emitted op carries a
  rounding contract (the architectural point of the family).
- **The verdict is nearly free ON DEVICE; the sync is the cost.**
  Jitted check kernel vs jitted eval: 1.037 vs 0.982 ms — the
  classify + atomic reduction + scalar memsets price at ~55 µs on
  65K entries. The check WRAPPER's 3.8 ms includes unjitted python
  dispatch and the mandatory device-to-host verdict read; a consumer
  embedding the check inside a jitted program and deferring the
  scalar read pays kernel price, not wrapper price.
- **Aliased accumulate carries the same ~2.5-3 ms boundary cost as
  the s64 twin** in this harness shape — consistent with the prior
  receipt's aliased-boundary observations, not a property of the
  interval arithmetic.
- **Tightness: at the ideal within display precision on generic
  rows.** matmul 16^3, random N(0,1)-scale intervals: device width /
  ideal width median AND max print 1.000 (the per-op directed
  rounding cost is ~1e-16 relative at width scale ~2 — invisible at
  three decimals; the ratio is exactly what the sandwich instrument
  bounds). The isolated rounding signal: the point-interval
  mirrored-cancellation dot 128 (ideal width exactly 0) shows device
  width 2.2e-13 — ~10^2.5 ulps-at-unit-scale of accumulated outward
  rounding over 128 hull-adds of ~unit products, the pure
  directed-rounding cost the straddle verdict exists to catch.
- **The fused-Pallas interval core compiles cleanly at its cap.**
  8192 elements × ~18 islands (0.347 ms/call) traced and compiled
  without tripping the slow-compile alarm; `IVL_HADAMARD_MAX_ELEMENTS`
  stays 8192 until a consumer needs more (the growth path is grid
  tiling, not a bigger unroll).

## Dispatch consequences (ENGINEERING #3)

The interval FFI lane is the DEFAULT device path for enclosure
contractions within the descriptor bounds — it is the only sound
device path, so dispatch is by soundness, not by race. The check
entry point is the consumer boundary of record; callers wanting the
kernel-side price embed it jitted and read the verdict scalars late.
The fused-Pallas interval hadamard-accumulate is receipt-backed at
its scope (elementwise, %128, <= 8192/call) for fused streaming
updates.
